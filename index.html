CONTROLS

    

      
Player 1

        
W A S DJump / back / crouch / forward

        
J  KButtons X / Y (punches)

        
N  MButtons A / B (kicks)

        
U  IButtons Z / C

        
Player 2

        
Arrow keysMove, jump, crouch

        
;  'Buttons X / Y

        
.  /Buttons A / B

        
[  ]Buttons Z / C

        
Gamepads work too. Esc or P pauses. B shows hit and hurt boxes, T changes the practice dummy. Hold back to block, as in MUGEN.

      
Kaelith (base fighter)

        
↓ ↘ → + X / YFrost Orb (X slow, Y fast)

        
→ ↓ ↘ + X / YRising Strike (anti-air)

        
↓ ↙ ← + A / BComet Kick

        
Y + BImmortal Rage (1000 power)

        
↓ + YUppercut launcher

        
↓ + BSweep (block low)

        
Debugging

        
SpaceCheat: full life and power

        
F2Cheat: CPU opponent to 1 life (not in 2-player)

        
F3Copy a diagnostic report (attach it to bug reports)

        
F4Overlay: state, anim, frame, velocity per fighter

        
Imported fighters use their own move lists. Open Fighters & Stages to load MUGEN characters and stages from a folder or ZIP.
